## What Is Dawn Aerowar?

Dawn Aerowar is an interactive animated film — a film first, not a traditional game. It blends 3D animation, hand-drawn 2D art, and full voice acting to tell the story of Zodiac 1, a fighter pilot on his final mission, driven by revenge against the Saal'Runa Empire that took everything from him. His job: escort a weapon no one should know exists, while the Empire's last remaining aces try to stop him.

The official website is [dawnaerowar.com](https://dawnaerowar.com/), where you'll find the full breakdown of the film, its team, and release details. The film launches on **Steam for Windows on November 10, 2026**.

## How You Watch It: Cinematic Mode and the Map

Dawn Aerowar gives you two ways to experience the story:

- **Cinematic mode** plays the film from start to finish, with no radio interruptions and no return to the map — a pure movie experience.
- **The player map** lets you choose each of the 12 episodes individually.

Either way, the pitch is simple: sit back, turn the volume up, and watch the story unfold.

## The Story: Zodiac 1 and the Aces of the Empire

The war in the air is staged in 3D and shot like a film, following one pilot on a last sortie across the entire Empire — from the northern front to the strait, the Aegis Sea, and the Capital. The intimate moments, from faces to cockpit interiors, are all hand-drawn 2D art.

Three aces have been sent to stop Zodiac, and each asks him the same question: do you even know what you're fighting for?

## Voices and the Original Soundtrack

Zodiac flies alone, and the war reaches him entirely through his cockpit radio — via a sharp, strangely well-informed AWACS operator he's never heard before. A professional voice cast performs every line, supported by more than thirty additional voices.

The soundtrack is 77 minutes of original music composed by Jose Pavli, written to picture. Expect fast-paced, epic scores featuring choirs, Bulgarian voices, classical guitar, and two original songs.
