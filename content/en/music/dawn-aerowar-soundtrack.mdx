## What Is the DAWN AEROWAR Soundtrack?

The first officially released piece of the DAWN AEROWAR soundtrack is a track called **"Bells of Dawn,"** composed by French composer Jose Pavli. It served as the music for the game's announcement teaser, and it was released as a standalone digital single on Bandcamp on September 26, 2021.

If you're trying to find out who scored DAWN AEROWAR or where to hear its music, this track is the confirmed starting point — everything below is drawn directly from the composer's official Bandcamp release page.

The full in-film score is 77 minutes of original music composed by Jose Pavli, according to the official Steam listing. It was written to picture and features choirs, Bulgarian voices, classical guitar, and two original songs.

## Who Is Jose Pavli?

Jose Pavli is a French composer for film, TV, and video games, based in London. According to his official biography, he began studying classical guitar, drums, orchestral percussion, and music theory at age four. He went on to study at the Conservatoire National de Marseille and ENJMIN (a French school focused on video games), before attending the National Film and Television School (NFTS) in London.

His discography shows he's no stranger to game music — his Bandcamp page lists releases including the *TimeSplitters Rewind* original soundtrack (November 2025) and *At World's End* (January 2025). His work on the DAWN AEROWAR soundtrack fits that pattern of orchestral, cinematic game scoring; the track itself is tagged with descriptors like orchestral, epic, hybrid, choir, and soundtrack.

## "Bells of Dawn": Lyrics and Sound

"Bells of Dawn" runs about two minutes and 41 seconds and features lead vocals by Erin Austen, backed by a full choir. The lyrics are short and evocative:

> Sons of Freedom,
> The time has come,
> Ring the Bells of Dawn.
>
> For us, they come,
> Ring the Bells of Dawn.

That martial, anthem-like tone suggests the track was written to set the emotional stakes of the announcement teaser — a call to action built around the imagery of dawn bells.
