Dawn Aerowar is an interactive animated film about one pilot's final mission, and its character roster is deliberately small and intimate. The official site names exactly one on-screen protagonist — Zodiac 1 — plus a faceless AWACS operator and three enemy aces from the Saal'Runa Empire. Everything in the nearly two-hour film flows through those voices and cockpits.

## Zodiac 1: A Pilot Powered by Revenge

Zodiac 1 is the heart of the story. According to the [official Dawn Aerowar website](https://dawnaerowar.com/), he lost his family, his home, and his purpose to the Empire, and only one thing keeps him flying: revenge.

His last sortie takes him across the entire Saal'Runa Empire — from the northern front, past the strait and the Aegis Sea, all the way to the Capital. He escorts a weapon that no one is supposed to know exists, which makes his flight the last thread the Free Nations have left in a war they are losing.

## The AWACS Operator: One Voice for the Whole War

Zodiac flies alone, and the only human contact he has is a radio voice. The AWACS operator aboard the surveillance flight is someone Zodiac has never heard before — and that anonymity is part of the character's design.

The official description paints a specific personality: the operator talks a lot, is sharp, and is strangely well informed. Through this single voice, Zodiac (and the viewer) experiences the entire scope of the war. It's a storytelling device reminiscent of classic radio-driven aerial narratives, where the controller on the ground becomes the co-lead.

## The Three Aces of the Saal'Runa Empire

Standing between Zodiac and the Capital are three aces — the last pilots still standing after years of war. The Empire has sent them to stop him, and each one poses the same haunting question:

- **Do you even know what you're fighting for?**

That repeated question is the thematic core of the film's antagonists. They aren't just obstacles; they're mirrors, forcing Zodiac to confront whether revenge is really a cause worth dying for.

The official Steam listing names the three aces: **Reaper**, the Name of Death, loves war more than peace; **Freya**, the Imperial Goddess, quotes philosophers while she shoots; and **Miracle**, the Guardian Angel, is known for the lives she saved rather than the ones she took.

## How the Characters Are Brought to Life

A professional voice cast performs every line in English, with more than thirty additional voices filling out roles from hardened pilots to ruthless commanders. Visually, the characters are rendered through a dual approach:

- **3D animation** stages and shoots every aerial sequence like a live-action film
- **Hand-drawn 2D art** handles the faces and cockpit interiors, giving intimate moments a personal, illustrated feel

With no live actors and no playable character dialogue trees, the emotional weight rests entirely on voice performance and animation — which is why the cast matters so much here.

## Why the Cast Is So Small on Purpose

Dawn Aerowar is a film first, structured as 12 episodes across nearly two hours, playable in Cinematic mode or selected individually from a map. The tight character list isn't a limitation — it's the design. By isolating Zodiac with one radio voice and three pursuing aces, the story keeps every conversation loaded with tension. For a project 186% funded by more than 600 Kickstarter backers, the focus on a lean, voice-driven ensemble is clearly a deliberate creative choice by director Jose Pavli and writer Matthew "FlyAwayNow" Nguyen.

## FAQ

**Who is the main character in Dawn Aerowar?**
The protagonist is Zodiac 1, a fighter pilot driven by revenge after the Saal'Runa Empire took his family, his home, and his purpose. He flies a final escort mission across the entire Empire.

**Who voices the characters in Dawn Aerowar?**
A professional English voice cast performs all lines, including Sam Hughes, Kira Buckland, Lara Sawalha, Blythe Melin, Sandra Espinoza, Mark Mullens, Jeremiah Foltz, and over thirty additional voices.

**Who are the aces in Dawn Aerowar?**
Reaper, Freya, and Miracle are the named aces of the Saal'Runa Empire sent to stop Zodiac 1. Each asks him the same question: do you even know what you're fighting for?

**When does Dawn Aerowar release?**
Dawn Aerowar launches on Steam for Windows on November 10, 2026, with English voices and subtitles in English, French, Italian, German, and Spanish.
