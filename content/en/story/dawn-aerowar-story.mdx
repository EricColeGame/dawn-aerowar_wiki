## What the Dawn Aerowar Story Is About

Dawn Aerowar tells the story of a fighter pilot known as Zodiac 1, a man flying his final mission across a world that has already lost the war. The Saal'Runa Empire has crushed every resistance, and the Free Nations stand on the brink of annihilation. Zodiac 1 is the last one still flying.

His motivation is simple and devastating: the Empire took his family, his home, and his purpose. The only thing keeping him in the cockpit is revenge. On this final sortie, he escorts a weapon that no one is supposed to know exists, and the only thing standing between him and the end of his flight are the aces of the Saal'Runa Empire — the last pilots still standing after years of war.

## The Route of the Last Sortie

Zodiac 1's mission spans the entire Empire. According to the official [Dawn Aerowar website](https://dawnaerowar.com/), the flight takes him from the northern front to the strait, then across the Aegis Sea, and finally to the Capital. Each leg of the journey is staged and animated in 3D, shot like a feature film rather than rendered as gameplay.

That structure gives the story a clear sense of progression — a single, continuous push deeper into enemy territory, with the stakes rising as he gets closer to his destination.

## The Three Aces Who Hunt Zodiac 1

The emotional core of the story comes from Zodiac 1's enemies. Three aces have been sent to stop him, and each of them confronts him with the same question: *do you even know what you're fighting for?*

That recurring question is what elevates the Dawn Aerowar story beyond a simple revenge tale. Zodiac 1 flies for vengeance alone, and his opponents — the Empire's last surviving pilots — force him to reckon with what that vengeance actually costs.

## A War Heard Through One Voice

Zodiac 1 flies alone, and the only connection he has to the wider war is his cockpit radio. Through it, he hears an AWACS operator he has never met before — someone talkative, sharp, and strangely well informed. Through that single voice, the entire scope of the war reaches him.

The production backs this up with a full professional voice cast: hardened pilots, ruthless commanders, and more than thirty additional voices, every line fully performed. Hand-drawn 2D art handles the faces and cockpit scenes, while 3D animation covers the aerial combat.

## How You Experience the Story

Dawn Aerowar is an interactive animated film, not a traditional game. You sit back, turn the volume up, and watch the story unfold. It runs nearly two hours across 12 episodes.

- **Cinematic mode** plays the whole film start to finish, with no radio and no map — the closest thing to a "War Room" pure-movie experience
- **The player** lets you choose each episode from a map
- Controls support keyboard, mouse, or gamepad

The original soundtrack, composed by Jose Pavli, runs 77 minutes and was written to picture, blending choirs, Bulgarian voices, and classical guitar, plus two original songs. The project was funded on Kickstarter at 186% by more than 600 backers.

## Release Details

Dawn Aerowar launches on Steam for Windows on November 10, 2026. English voice acting is included, with subtitles in English, French, Italian, German, and Spanish.
